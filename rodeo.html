The Rodeo · Stetson Bowl

Where the best come to ride.

British Columbia's largest rodeo, and it's invitational — 96 of the world's top cowboys and cowgirls, four championship events, and a purse north of $300,000, settled over five days at the Cloverdale Fairgrounds.

The final day

It all comes down to Monday.

Five days of rides funnel down to one Monday in the Stetson Bowl. The final day decides all four events — Bareback, Saddle Bronc, Bull Riding, and Ladies Barrel Racing — and with them the coveted title of Cloverdale Rodeo Champion.

Then comes the part you stand up for — the victory lap, hat off to a grandstand that's been loud since Thursday.
