Before you go

FAQs

Quick answers for the long weekend — the stuff people actually search for at 9 pm the night before.

Representative questions — final answers come from CREA's confirmed policies.

What can I bring through the gates?

Bag and item rules are set by CREA and can differ between the open grounds and ticketed venues like the Stetson Bowl and the Longhorn Saloon. The live site carries the confirmed policy here, in plain language.

Can I leave and come back the same day?

Re-entry depends on your ticket type. The live site states CREA's confirmed re-entry policy for each ticket, right where you buy it.

What happens if it rains?

It's five days outdoors on a BC long weekend — dress in layers and plan for changeable spring weather. Any weather-related updates would be posted here and on the schedule page.

Is the fair good for young kids?

The fair side of the grounds is built for families — midway and carnival rides, agriculture exhibits, the Indigenous Village, and family attractions. Ride height and age specifics would be listed with the confirmed midway details.

What accessibility services are on the grounds?

That answer belongs to CREA — the live site would carry their confirmed information on accessible viewing, parking, washrooms, and services at the Cloverdale Fairgrounds.
