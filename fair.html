The Country Fair · Est. 1888

An original kind of country.

Cloverdale has been throwing this party since 1888. Five days on the Victoria Day long weekend, 80,000+ friends and neighbours, and one country fair holding it all together.

On the grounds

The fair

Midway rides, 40+ food trucks, agriculture exhibits, the Indigenous Village, and family attractions — walkable in an afternoon, better after dark.

Midway & rides

The midway, all five days

Carnival rides, games, and a fair that keeps finding reasons to make you look up — in 2026, Global FMX riders flew over it all.

Food & fair nights

40+ food trucks. Come hungry.

More than forty food trucks line the grounds, so nobody agrees on dinner and nobody has to. When the sun drops, the whole fair turns into one big evening out.

Agriculture & community

Indigenous Village & agriculture exhibits

Agriculture exhibits, family attractions, and the Indigenous Village share the grounds all five days — the quieter side of the fair, and for plenty of families the best one.

Representative — 2027 exhibits and programming are announced by CREA closer to the fair.

Seven stages

The music

100+ artists across seven stages in five days — headline sets under the open sky, a packed saloon, and a garden stage for the quiet ones.

The big stage

CR Main Stage

The night's biggest sets, played to the biggest crowd on the grounds.

When the light goes down over the fairgrounds, this is where everyone drifts. The headliners roll out as the sky turns, and the crowd stretches back into the dark.

The saloon

Longhorn Saloon

Loud, close, and packed to the rails — the honky-tonk heart of the fair.

Shoulder to shoulder, singing every word. And when you're ready to bring the night down a notch, the Hops & Vines Garden stage keeps the music at porch volume, string lights included.

Who's played Cloverdale

Past performers include Brett Kissel, Cooper Alan, The Strumbellas, Gord Bamford, The Road Hammers, Corb Lund, George Canyon, Terri Clark, 54-40, Washboard Union, Marty Stuart, Aaron Pritchett, and Johnny Reid.

Past editions only — the 2027 lineup is announced by CREA. No artist on this page is a 2027 booking.

After dark

Every light in the air

Fair nights here end the same way every year — a few thousand phone lights inside the Longhorn, and one last look up before the walk to the gate.
