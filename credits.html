Whose work you're seeing.

This concept exists to show the Cloverdale Rodeo and Exhibition Association what their redesigned site could feel like — using their own event, told through their own photography.

Event photography — © Cloverdale Rodeo and Exhibition Association

All rodeo, fair and music photographs on this concept are from CREA's own coverage of the May 2026 Cloverdale Rodeo & Country Fair, hotlinked from cloverdalerodeo.com. They appear here, within this unaffiliated concept, purely to present the proposed redesign with the event's real imagery. All rights remain with CREA and its photographers. Should CREA prefer, any image can be removed on request.
